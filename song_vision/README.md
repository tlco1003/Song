# SONG-Vision v1 — local original singing-mouth model (research prototype)

This repository contains **original trainable PyTorch model code**, not an external
video-generation API. The **model has no trained weights in Git** and cannot
produce a convincing singing video until suitable authorized data have been
prepared, training completed and the resulting quality independently evaluated.

## v13.1: observed pupil / iris-centre motion in the emotion branch

The [2D projected iris estimator](projected_iris_v13.py) adds **four measured iris-center movement proxies** (left/right eye x/y relative to observed eye corners). The emotion stage now also trains a small iris motion head and separately validates measured 2D iris motion. **This is not real 3D eye gaze, pupil dilation, iris physics or inferred mental emotion**; blinking and gaze cannot be uniquely determined from vocal loudness. Missing/invalid iris source labels are masked out rather than fabricated. Earlier v13/v12 and other research checkpoints are retained.

## v13: RUNNABLE four-stage pronunciation / prosody / emotion / face composer

This new *code*, not only a design document, is in [the four-stage PyTorch trainer](staged_face_v13.py), [human-reviewed phone-time contract](phoneme_targets_v13.py), [official 120-take runner](external_ravdess_v13.py), and [actual test suite](test_staged_face_v13.py). Stage 1 articulation learns lip/jaw visible motion, plus **13 Korean consonant/5 vowel phone classes only if genuinely human-reviewed phone timestamps exist**. Stage 2 learns vocal dynamics/residuals, stage 3 eyes/brow/cheek/skin and RAVDESS *portrayed* emotion, and stage 4 trains the fused decoder. Existing model weights remain in encrypted vault and new weights get their own run-specific encrypted archive. Official RAVDESS English footage has no annotated Korean phonemes, so phone classification is **untrained there**; no invented phone labels. No sound generation or full-face photorealistic skin renderer. Full guide: [v13 research code contract](../docs/SONG_VISION_V13_ACTUAL_FOUR_STAGE_CODE.md).

## v12: SAME VOWELS AND PHRASES — NORMAL VS STRONG SINGING EXPRESSION

The [research protocol](../docs/SONG_VISION_V12_INTENSITY_VOWEL_FACE.md) adds matching RAVDESS normal/strong emotional expression performances of **the same actor, same emotion, same sung statement, same repetition**. Earlier trials used only the normal condition; the v12 [dataset and study runner](external_ravdess_intensity_v12.py) selects 120 real takes from 3 singers (80 train clips from actors 01/02; 40 holdout clips from actor 03), verifying MD5 and exact condition pairs. It trains existing SONG voice-to-face v11 model from scratch with BOTH conditions, reports unseen singer error separately for normal and strong expression, and preserves a separate encrypted checkpoint. Emotional intensity is **NOT measured muscle force, phonation effort or calibrated dB SPL**. Common vowel shapes vary by pitch, singing style and person; 5 Roman vowel labels do not cover consonants, all languages or all sounds. No acoustic synthesis, generated vocals or commercial inference.

## v11: READY-MADE SONG VOCALS -> PRONUNCIATION/INTENSITY FACE MOVEMENT (NOT VOICE SYNTHESIS)

[Voice-to-Face v11 research guide](../docs/SONG_VISION_VOICE_TO_FACE_V11.md). The new [8-channel acoustic measurements](vocal_acoustics.py) preserve actual singer loudness dBFS, level slope, spectral attacks, frequency centroid and band ratios, noise/tonal spectral shape, synchronized to 25fps camera face frames. The [SONG-owned PyTorch model](voice_to_face_v11.py) uses both 210ms log-Mel spectral context and 610ms vocal dynamics to learn source-video mouth image + 38D observed visible face motions. No deterministic 'loud = open mouth' rule, voice model, voice synthesis, or verified phoneme recognizer. Lyrics and aligned phoneme truth are needed for strict Korean viseme accuracy evaluation. The [official research runner](external_ravdess_voice_to_face_v11.py) uses RAVDESS 01+02 training, 03 holdout, writes encrypted model only to a separately versioned research vault. Existing models retained, paid customer jobs still fail-closed.

## v10: singer vocal sounds vs visible mouth, jaw, eye and skin dynamics

A separate SONG-built [facial-articulation network](facial_articulation_v10.py) learns audio-conditioned lip pixels and **38 measured face-surface motion proxies** (existing 12 jaw/lips/cheeks + 26 eye, eyebrow, nose-side and perioral expression coefficients). The frozen offline Face Landmarker supplies per-frame supervision, not physiological EMG measurements. Consecutive 40ms frame motion and 320ms mismatched-vocal counterexamples provide timing supervision; voice does **not** uniquely determine eye blinks or the sound generated by vocal folds. Research runner [v10](external_ravdess_face_articulation_v10.py) trains on official CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 RAVDESS actors 01+02, holds out actor 03, and encrypts checkpoints into the existing research vault. Existing v2/v9 research weights and production SONG customer job gate are unchanged. See [v10 guide](../docs/SONG_VISION_AUDIOLIP_SKIN_V10.md).

## v9: singing jaw, cheek and lip JOINT surface-motion training

[v9 face geometry research guide](../docs/SONG_VISION_FULL_FACE_JOINT_V9.md) documents the new self-authored network. The [frozen offline 478-point/52-expression teacher](face_surface_motion.py) provides real frame labels; the [joint PyTorch student](joint_face_training.py) shares voice features between lip image and **jaw/chin + cheek surface motion** objectives. Predicted surface motion is also fed back into the mouth decoder. The current 64px mouth decoder **does not produce full cheek/jaw photo pixels**. Surface landmarks do not represent actual zygomatic or mandible bone structure. The research-only [runner](external_ravdess_face_joint.py) uses strict RAVDESS licenses, signer disjoint holdout and encrypted checkpoint preservation. Existing v2 checkpoints are preserved; optional owner-restored checkpoints can initialize the mouth subnet while training the new motion head.

## v8: new external singers + independent held-out singer evaluation

The verified [RAVDESS three-actor research runner](external_ravdess_multisinger.py) **trains with actors 01 and 02, then validates only on previously unseen actor 03**; unlike prior same-actor validation, this split tests one new performer. The source is the official CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 Zenodo record and each actor archive is verified against its official MD5. All trained research weights and reports are encrypted for the existing owner-held key and copied to `research/song-vision-vault` as independent `.svault` files, not discarded. This is NOT commercial training or validated singing lip-sync. Guide: [docs/SONG_VISION_INDEPENDENT_SINGER_RETRAIN_V8.md](../docs/SONG_VISION_INDEPENDENT_SINGER_RETRAIN_V8.md).

## Research model preservation (NEW)

Training on RAVDESS is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and cannot feed paid SONG jobs. The newer research pipeline **encrypts and preserves every completed checkpoint**, including the training report, in the `research/song-vision-vault` branch and a separate 90-day Actions artifact. The private X25519 key is held by the owner outside GitHub, never by the runner. If it is lost, restoration is impossible. See [the research checkpoint vault guide](../docs/SONG_VISION_RESEARCH_CHECKPOINT_VAULT_V1.md). Previous 36-epoch weights had no artifact and cannot be recovered; a fresh research run will create a backup. Raw singer videos, vocals and face crops remain transient.

## v7: extended training rounds on 20 authorized research-only singing takes

The [diverse research runner](external_ravdess_diverse_retrain.py) now requests **up to 36 epochs** (previously 10) with **minimum 20 epochs, patience 10 and min-delta 0.0002**. The model re-trains **from scratch** because the noncommercial research checkpoint was deliberately destroyed after earlier GitHub Actions jobs. Early stopping ends training only after the minimum when validation does not improve materially. Results are checked against the previous ten-epoch run on the same fixed actor/take split; this cannot prove phoneme-correct singing or commercial readiness. See [extended retraining report](../docs/SONG_VISION_EXTENDED_RETRAIN_V7.md).

## v6: masked-reference training to investigate audio-conditioning failure

The experimental [masked_retrain.py](masked_retrain.py) network removes the central mouth from the reference image **before** the encoder sees it, then fuses an explicit 80-bin Mel acoustic embedding with skin/face context. It learns inpainting from matched singer/audio frames, evaluates matched-versus-shuffled audio losses, reference-copy baseline and audio sensitivity, and saves a distinct **unvalidated** checkpoint. The isolated RAVDESS research pipeline is in [external_ravdess_retrain.py](external_ravdess_retrain.py); its research weights and video data are removed after the temporary CI runner finishes. Results do not authorize commercial use. Full technical guide: [docs/SONG_VISION_MASKED_RETRAIN_V6.md](../docs/SONG_VISION_MASKED_RETRAIN_V6.md).

## v4: Korean visemes, manual vocal alignment and controlled lip geometry

The offline [local annotation editor](annotation_editor.html) lets an operator listen to an isolated vocal, mark individual Korean-syllable boundaries, insert explicit rests and export a **file-hash-bound** timing JSON without uploading it. The Python [viseme_timeline.py](viseme_timeline.py) validates the reviewer, timing and audio hash and generates conservative opening/width targets for all 21 Korean vowels with approximate bilabial closure and short transitions. The existing [face_geometry.py](face_geometry.py) supports optional **--timeline**; without it, the legacy RMS-only warp remains available. No automatic phoneme recognition, singing-accurate lip sync or trained customer face swapping is claimed. Full Korean usage guide: [docs/SONG_VISION_KOREAN_VISEME_V4.md](../docs/SONG_VISION_KOREAN_VISEME_V4.md).

## v3: facial surface geometry and offline lip-warp experiments

See [`docs/SONG_VISION_FACE_GEOMETRY_V3.md`](../docs/SONG_VISION_FACE_GEOMETRY_V3.md). New commands: `python -m song_vision.face_geometry analyze-face` (eye, nose, lip, chin surface coordinates) and `python -m song_vision.face_geometry warp-video` (RMS-driven **approximate** mouth displacement over an authorized singer video). This geometry preview cannot reproduce exact Korean phonemes or determine true skull/jaw/teeth/tongue anatomy; model weights for the landmark detector are a separately downloaded Google offline model, **not** a newly trained SONG model. Existing custom neural net training remains separate.

## Architecture

- FFmpeg decodes the **isolated vocal track** locally to mono 16 kHz.
- Pure PyTorch STFT + triangular Mel filterbank produce 80-channel/10-ms features.
- OpenCV detects one mostly frontal face in a **consented singing-video** training clip.
- A 96x96 face is reduced to a 64x64 lower-face mouth region.
- The original `SingingMouthNet` uses a 2D image encoder, 1D vocal encoder,
  FiLM-conditioned features and 2D transpose-convolution decoder.
- Training pairs source/reference and target frames from the **same singer**.
- Rendering reuses the stage video frames and only replaces the lower-face ROI.
- FFmpeg merges the final visual result with the original music mix into an MP4.

This is a **research baseline**. It does not implement LivePortrait, MuseTalk,
a full facial foundation model, trained customer-identity transfer, or high-grade
diffusion video generation. There is no facial-recognition identification.

## v2: synchronization-aware real singer training pipeline

The executable `python -m song_vision.real_training` workflow accepts *actual* singer video and its synchronized isolated vocals, normalizes 25fps input, separates train/validation by clip, reports audio-sensitivity diagnostics, and saves the best checkpoint. A two-clip **synthetic** end-to-end test exercises the complete data path. It does not supply a real licensed singer dataset or validated video quality.

See [`docs/SONG_VISION_SYNC_TRAINING_V2.md`](../docs/SONG_VISION_SYNC_TRAINING_V2.md) for exact commands, manifest schema, licensing/consent safeguards and limitations.

## Local setup (Python 3.11+ and ffmpeg on PATH)

```sh
python -m venv .venv
. .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r song_vision/requirements.txt
python -m unittest discover -s song_vision -p 'test_*.py' -v
```

### 1. Prepare synchronized and authorized singing footage

Create a **private, operator-verified** consent JSON file for the singer:
`{"approved": true, "scope": "train_singing", "subjectId": "singer-owner-approved"}`.
This JSON is only an application gate: the operator must separately verify the
person's actual approval, rights, capture permissions, and retention policy.
Do not commit consent documents or face images to Git.

```sh
python -m song_vision prepare \
  --video /private/source_singer_video.mp4 \
  --vocals /private/same_time_isolated_vocals.wav \
  --dataset song_vision/datasets \
  --clip-id consented-singer-001 \
  --seconds 30 \
  --consent-record /private/train-consent.json
```

The audio must genuinely correspond to the video, aligned at time zero. If
there is no isolated vocal track, generate it separately or use authorized raw
vocals; instrumental-heavy inputs can degrade results.

### 2. Train original weights from scratch

```sh
python -m song_vision train \
  --dataset song_vision/datasets \
  --checkpoint song_vision/checkpoints/singing-v1.pt \
  --epochs 30 --batch-size 16 --learning-rate 0.0003 --device cpu
```

For CUDA hardware use `--device cuda` with a properly installed GPU-enabled
PyTorch build. **Training on the source video alone does not guarantee general
performance, accurate lip sync, identity stability or visual realism.**
Perform held-out singer/video evaluation before calling it successful.

### 3. Experimental 10-second MP4 inference

```sh
python -m song_vision render \
  --template /private/stage_performance.mp4 \
  --vocals /private/song_isolated_vocals.wav \
  --mix /private/final_song.mp3 \
  --checkpoint song_vision/checkpoints/singing-v1.pt \
  --output song_vision/outputs/demo.mp4 \
  --seconds 10 --start 0 --device cpu
```

The result writes `demo.mp4` and `demo.quality.json` locally, with the latter
explicitly marked **research_only_not_commercially_validated**. Output is gated
on at least 60% frontal-face detection, but passing this threshold is not proof
of acceptable lip-sync quality.

### Optional *experimental 2D face image compositor* (NOT neural face-swap)

```sh
python -m song_vision render \
  --template /private/stage_performance.mp4 \
  --vocals /private/song_isolated_vocals.wav \
  --mix /private/final_song.mp3 \
  --checkpoint song_vision/checkpoints/singing-v1.pt \
  --output song_vision/outputs/experimental-face.mp4 \
  --seconds 10 \
  --identity-photo /private/authorized-customer-face.png \
  --consent-record /private/face-swap-consent.json
```

Consent JSON for this specific path must have `approved:true`,
`scope:"face_swap_prototype"`, and a nonempty `subjectId`.
The operator MUST verify actual consent. **This overlays a static portrait
using OpenCV seamlessClone; it is NOT yet a trained identity-transfer neural
network. It can look visibly artificial and must not be sold as finished
face-swap AI.**

## Connection with existing SONG

`GET /api/video-ai/readiness` exposes a read-only engine status. Existing
song choice, server price authority, payment lockout, private music prompts,
delivery protections, and checkout behavior remain unchanged. There is
**no customer upload endpoint, no public generation job endpoint, no automatic
commercial fulfillment, no external inference provider and no secret keys**
in this v1 branch.

A **future**, separately validated self-hosted service may expose authenticated
internal generation jobs after obtaining trained weights, durable queue, media
storage, verified consent, and quantitative plus human quality checks. Until
that point `canAcceptCustomerJobs=false`.

## Research background (methods are not copied)

All quality claims about these methods belong to those publications, **not**
to SONG-Vision v1. The current checkpoint must be trained and evaluated on
authorized data before claiming any comparable capability.
